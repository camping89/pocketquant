## Owner decisions added during adjudication

- **D7 (manual action queue).** The owner asked to re-check the claim that a queued close could stay stuck. Checked in the public Exness spec (`https://www.exness-api.com/exness_api.yaml`, fetched 2026-11-01): `closePosition` is unavailable only when the instrument `trade_mode` is `trading_disabled` (spec lines 839-866) and stays available in `close_only`; it may still fail asynchronously with `MARKET_SESSION_CLOSED`, `MARKET_TRADE_DISABLED` or `MARKET_NO_QUOTES`. `cancelPendingOrder` is unavailable only when `trade_mode` is `trading_disabled` (spec lines 634-650) and has no market-session async errors. The blocker in our system is our own live-trading fence (`_ORDER_OPS`, `core/infra/exness/exness_client.py:46`). Decision: `closePosition` and `cancelPendingOrder` are not fenced by the live flag (they only reduce exposure); `openPosition` and `placePendingOrder` stay fenced. Every attempt to apply a manual action clears it and records its outcome (`accepted` or `rejected:<code>`) in the snapshot ring and the subscription row; the owner retries if needed. The only reason an action stays queued is that the trader does not hold the lease or has no price yet (D6 still holds). No withdraw route.
- **D8 (read access).** The new reads (`GET /subscriptions/{id}/snapshot`, `GET /subscriptions/{id}/orders`, the enriched subscription list) and the existing positions and trades reads require the admin token. The web sends the token on these reads.
- **D9 (freshness column).** Dropped. No spike measurement for it.
